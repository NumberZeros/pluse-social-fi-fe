# Why Pulse Social

Web2 platforms take 30%+, own your audience, and control the algorithm. Creators deserve better.

## The Old Way (Web2 Problems)

### Platform Risk

Your account can be banned or suspended without warning. Years of content and followers gone in an instant.

### Zero Ownership

You don't own your username, content, or followers. The platform owns everything and can change rules anytime.

### Value Extraction

Platforms make billions from your content and data. Creators earn pennies while platforms take 30–50% cuts.

### Data Silos

Your social graph is locked in. Can't take followers to other platforms. Start from zero every time.

### Censorship

Arbitrary content moderation. No appeals. No transparency. What you can say is controlled by corporations.

## The Pulse Way

### Supporter Shares

Fans buy shares to support you and unlock exclusive content — real utility, not speculation.

### Direct Earnings

Tips land in your wallet. Share support flows to your on-chain pool — no platform taking 30%.

### Own Your Audience

On-chain profile and followers you control. Your community travels with you.

### Exclusive Content Gating

Post supporter-only updates. Access verified on-chain before content renders.

### Built on Solana

Fast, cheap transactions. The creator coin ecosystem is growing on Solana.

## Why Solana?

- **Blazing Fast** — 400ms block times. Posts appear instantly.
- **Ultra Cheap** — Low transaction fees make posting, tipping, and supporting creators affordable.
- **Global Scale** — 65,000+ TPS capacity for millions of concurrent users.

## Creator Economics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Tips to wallet | 100% |
| Platform cut on tips | 0% |
| Core product loop | 1 (Supporter Shares) |
| Exclusive posts | Unlimited |

Tips have 0% platform fee. When you sell Supporter Shares, 10% stays in the creator's pool vault (not a platform cut) to keep the supporter pool healthy.
