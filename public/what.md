# What is Pulse Social

Pulse helps creators earn on Solana. Fans buy **Supporter Shares** to unlock exclusive content. Tips go to your wallet; share support flows to your on-chain pool.

## Core Features

### Supporter Shares

Fans back you on-chain. Early supporters pay less as your community grows. Supporters unlock your exclusive posts.

### Direct Tips

Send SOL to creators in one click. 100% goes to their wallet — no platform cut.

### On-chain Profile & Feed

Your profile, followers, and posts live on Solana. Build an audience you own.

### IPFS Content

Public preview on IPFS; full exclusive content is stored separately and fetched only after on-chain access is verified.

## How It Works

1. **Create profile** — Connect your wallet and claim your on-chain username.
2. **Launch Supporter Shares** — Initialize your supporter pool from the Dashboard.
3. **Post exclusive content** — Share public updates or gate posts to supporters only.
4. **Fans support & unlock** — Supporters buy shares, unlock your feed, and can tip you.

## How It's Built

| Layer | Technology | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Blockchain | Solana | Fast, low-cost transactions |
| Storage | IPFS via Pinata | Decentralized post storage |
| Access | On-chain shares | Verified supporter access |

## MVP Scope

Pulse Social v1.1 focuses on one product loop: fans buy Supporter Shares → unlock supporters-only posts → creators earn on-chain. Marketplace, governance, groups, and subscription tiers are deferred post-MVP.

Live deployment: [pulsesol.xyz](https://pulsesol.xyz/) on Solana Devnet.
