🚀 Pulse Social v1.1.0 is live on Solana

We just shipped a major update to Pulse Social — a creator-first social platform where fans support creators through on-chain Supporter Shares.

What's new in v1.1.0:

💎 Supporter Shares MVP
Fans buy shares to unlock exclusive content. Creators earn directly — no platform cut on tips. Cash out anytime.

📊 Creator Dashboard
3-step onboarding wizard: set up profile → launch share pool → publish your first post. Real-time stats on supporters, earnings, and engagement.

🔒 Content Gating
Supporters-only posts with on-chain verification. Blur + CTA for non-supporters. IPFS-hosted media via Pinata.

📱 Mobile-First UX
Bottom navigation, responsive footer, WalletConnect for iOS Safari — connect your wallet from any device.

🔍 SEO & Discovery
Dynamic OG images, sitemap, schema markup, and llms.txt for better search and social sharing.

We intentionally scoped v1.1.0 to one core loop: support → unlock → earn. Everything else (marketplace, governance, groups) is on the roadmap.
